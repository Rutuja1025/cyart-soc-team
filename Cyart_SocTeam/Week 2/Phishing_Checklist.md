\[ ] Email Headers: Confirm From vs. Return-Path and check SPF/DKIM status.



\[ ] Link Reputation: Check the URL on VirusTotal or URLScan.io.



\[ ] Patient Zero: Identify which users opened the email or clicked links.



\[ ] Malware Analysis: If there is an attachment, run it in a sandbox (Cuckoo or Hybrid Analysis).



\[ ] Containment: Block the sender IP and the destination URL globally.
